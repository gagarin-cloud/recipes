After it installs, tell the server its own address, which is its OAuth issuer: gg deploy PROJECT/mcp:8080 IMAGE --env PORT=8080 --env TZ=UTC --env NODE_ENV=production --env PUBLIC_URL=https://ADDRESS, with IMAGE and ADDRESS as gg status PROJECT shows them. Until then, sign-in fails. The telegram input is API_ID and API_HASH from my.telegram.org; the encryption input is a KEY of at least 32 characters (openssl rand -hex 32).
